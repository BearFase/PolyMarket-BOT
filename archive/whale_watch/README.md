# Archived Whale Watch research

This directory contains the retired whale scanner, consensus experiment,
refresh helper, verification scripts, and old scanner launcher. The approach
attempted to discover and follow large traders but could not reliably identify
Polymarket US app wallets or support trustworthy performance claims.

It was replaced by canonical game-linked research observations and immutable
simulation tracks. Nothing here is imported, scheduled, or launched by the
active application. Historical `whale_data.json` and scanner databases remain
preserved locally and were not deleted.
